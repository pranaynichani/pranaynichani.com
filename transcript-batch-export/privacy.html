TRANSCRIPT BATCH EXPORT — PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026.

Transcript Batch Export does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

The plugin runs entirely inside Adobe Premiere Pro on your own computer. It makes no network requests of any kind. There is no account, no login, no telemetry, no analytics and no crash reporting.

What the plugin reads

Only what it needs to do the export, and only from the project you have open: sequence and clip names, bin names, project name, transcript data that Premiere has already generated, markers, frame rate and timecode.

What the plugin writes

  1. Your exported files, written only into the output folder you choose yourself with the system folder picker.
  2. A settings file (settings.json) in the plugin's own private data folder, provided by Adobe's UXP runtime. It contains your checkbox choices, filename template, saved presets, and a reference to your last output folder. It never leaves your computer. Uninstalling the plugin removes it.

Third parties

The plugin itself shares nothing with anyone. If you purchased it through the Adobe Creative Cloud Marketplace, that purchase was handled by Adobe and their payment provider FastSpring under their own privacy policies. We receive only aggregate sales information from Adobe, never your payment details.

Children

Transcript Batch Export is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published here with an updated date.
