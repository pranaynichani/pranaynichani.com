Story seeker, systems builder.

I'm a Toronto-based post-production specialist and editor with fifteen years in documentary filmmaking, commercial production, and narrative storytelling — for broadcasters and studios including the CBC, the National Film Board of Canada, Vice, Banger Films, and Blue Ant Media.

My work lives in the space most people never see: taking a documentary from terabytes of raw footage to a finished film. Prepping projects for edit. Building and managing workflows. Organizing footage, syncing multicam, managing media across features and eight-part series. Cutting assemblies, delivering for final sound and online, coordinating translators and subtitle pipelines, troubleshooting whatever breaks at 11pm before a mix.

I also cut. Editor credits include the features Illusion BOUND and Eye of the Hurricane (additional editor), the TIFF-IFFSA-winning short It Happens to Us, and Transfer, which won Best Editing at the International British Film Festival 2020 — alongside years of commercial and branded work as editor, DP, and motion graphics artist.

Here's what I believe: knowing the tools doesn't make you less of a storyteller. The edit suite, the workflow, the machinery of post — they're not the opposite of craft, they're the instrument of it. The more completely you know what your tools can do, the more of the story you can reach. I've built my career, and now my teaching, on that idea.

From 2022 to 2024 I was a professor of Non-Fiction Editing at Seneca College's Documentary Film Institute (Seneca@York) — the same program I graduated from in 2017 — until the program was paused amid Canada's cap on incoming international students. The teaching hasn't stopped, though: I still train assistant editors and editorial interns on my productions. More about my teaching →
